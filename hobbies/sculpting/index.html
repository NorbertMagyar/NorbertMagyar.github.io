Sculpting


I have always admired sculptures and stonemasonry, and will happily spend a few hours every now and then in museums admiring these works of art. Thankfully I have got to see many of the world's best sculptures over the years. Some of my favourite pieces of art and museums or galleries are the following:
  • Winged victory of Samothraki, Louvre, Paris
  • Venus Victrix, Galleria Borghese, Rome
  • Molossian Hound statue, British Museum, London
  • "Obsession", Beaux-Arts, Lille
  • Perseus slaying Medusa, Carlsberg Glyptotek, Copenhagen
  • "Adoration I" and other works by Stephan Sinding, Carlsberg Glyptotek, Copenhagen
  • Sarcophagi of Helena_and Constantina, Vatican Museum, Rome
  • Moschophoros, part of the "Persinal rubble" find, Acropolis Museum, Athens
  • The amazing works of the contemporary sculptor Jago

A few years back (2022-ish), rather optimistically, I have decided to try and sculpt, without prior training or experience. So I took a block of AAC (autoclaved aerated concrete) and started sawing and chiseling it. This is a very soft material that can be easily sawn with a wood handsaw and easily worked using rasps and sanding, in other words an ideal choice for a complete beginner. The aim was none other but to try and reproduce the famous Venus of Milo ancient greek statue. For this, I have first studied in detail a 3D model of the statue on my laptop, and then drew the projected outlines of the statue on the block.
